About Us

RAKHI BALA'S HINDI TUTORIAL (LIVE ONLINE CLASS)

Rakhi Bala's Hindi Tutorial is a trusted live online learning centre dedicated to teaching Hindi to students of all ages and skill levels. Based in Periakottai, Tamil Nadu, we bridge the language gap for students across India who wish to learn Hindi effectively through engaging, interactive live sessions led by an experienced and passionate tutor. Our highly rated programme — scoring an impressive 4.9 out of 5 — reflects our unwavering commitment to student success and personalised attention.

We offer flexible class timings seven days a week from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, making it easy for school students, working professionals, and homemakers to fit Hindi learning into their busy schedules. Whether you are a complete beginner or looking to improve your reading, writing, or spoken Hindi skills, Rakhi Bala's Hindi Tutorial provides a warm, structured, and results-driven learning environment right from your screen.

  • Exceptional 4.9/5 rating from 49 satisfied students and parents
  • Live interactive online classes available 7 days a week, 9 AM to 5 PM
  • Personalised teaching approach for all age groups and skill levels
